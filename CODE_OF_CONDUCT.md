# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and critique ideas, not people.
- Give credit for prior work and cite sources.
- Respect privacy and do not publish others' data without consent.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, doxxing, and sustained
disruption are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and
other contributions that violate this code. Report concerns privately to the
maintainers listed in `CITATION.cff`. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
confidentially.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
